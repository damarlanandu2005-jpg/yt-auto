# Art prompts: backgrounds and detailed objects (draft 1, 29 Oct 2026)

Owner, 29 Oct: richer scenes like the reference Short "He Defused 41 Bombs Without Seeing Color": real places full of
objects, light where the place has light, many characters. Plan: AI pictures for BACKGROUNDS and DETAILED OBJECTS,
made once and saved in a library; characters stay our code stickman (same in every video, can move). The same prompts
are tried two ways and compared: the owner in ChatGPT/Gemini (by hand), and FLUX.1 schnell (Apache-2.0) on Kaggle.

Rules for every picture: flat cartoon (not realistic, so no YouTube AI label), no people, no text, no logos or brands.

## Style (put this first, every time)

> Flat 2D vector cartoon illustration, clean thick dark-grey outlines, soft muted colours with simple flat shading,
> like a modern animated explainer cartoon. Vertical 9:16 portrait. Eye-level view. Empty scene: NO people, NO
> animals, NO text, NO letters, NO logos. The lower third is open floor or ground with nothing on it, so characters
> can stand there. Calm, tidy, not cluttered in the middle. No frame, no border.
