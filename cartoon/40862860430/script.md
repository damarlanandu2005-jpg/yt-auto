## hook
What would happen to your body if you fell into the Mariana Trench?

## step1
You drop into the western Pacific Ocean, directly above a depth of 10,935 metres. Cold water triggers cold shock and uncontrolled gasping, which can kill within 2 minutes.

## answer
In short, cold shock and drowning can kill you in minutes. As you descend, rising water pressure can injure your gas-containing internal organs.
