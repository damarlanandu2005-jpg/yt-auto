How GPS Finds Your Location Using Satellite Timestamps

How can your phone find your exact location on Earth just by listening to the time?

Chapters:
0:00 The passive listener: why GPS satellites do not track you or receive signals from your phone
1:04 Turning time into space: how measuring radio travel times reveals your distance from an orbit
2:16 The sphere intersection: using trilateration to narrow your place on Earth to two points
3:15 The fourth satellite trick: how an extra signal fixes the cheap quartz clock in your pocket
4:25 Einstein in orbit: why satellite clocks tick faster and how engineers correct for relativity
6:00 Mapping the numbers: how raw coordinates turn into a moving blue dot on your screen
