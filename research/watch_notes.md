## Turned into production rules (proposed)

1. Opening: state the question and a short roadmap ("in this video: ...") in the first 20 seconds.
2. Voice: dramatic pauses at reveals and between scenes; slower speed on key/emotional lines; normal while explaining.
3. Background music: quiet, under the voice (ducked), licence CC0 or CC BY only.
4. Transition sounds (soft click/whoosh) at picture changes; CC0 only.
5. Step-by-step pictures: break each idea into small visual steps of the same subject (wide → close → detail).
6. On-screen key words timed to the voice (understandable without sound).
7. A topic with strong feeling matters more than perfect visuals (video 4; matches the research result).

Not possible at ₹0 now: realistic AI video clips (need a strong GPU) — and realistic AI scenes need YouTube's AI disclosure.
